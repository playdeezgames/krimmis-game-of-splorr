# Krimmis of SPLORR!!

A satirical holiday game in Odin and wasm, played in the browser: https://playdeezgames.github.io/krimmis-game-of-splorr/

The season is called Krimmis. Kris Krinkle sees everything, the Naughty List is a kill list, and you are a mall elf with 24 days to climb the Nice List by informing on your neighbours.

**Made with an AI agent.** The game, its sprites and its sounds were made by Claude (Claude Code) at the direction of the author, TheGrumpyGameDev.
